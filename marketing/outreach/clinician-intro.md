# Clinician intro: first personal email from Jake

**Use:** a short first email from Jake to an individual clinician in HubSpot who looks like a fit
for Stream: independent primary care, DPC, pediatrics, integrative/complex chronic, or SNF. Jake
sends it. Owen personalises each draft from the HubSpot record and never invents a detail.

**One idea:** your EHR keeps the record; Stream keeps the project, meaning the problem-by-problem
picture good clinicians keep by hand, which doesn't scale and doesn't transfer.

## Fields

| Field | Filled from | If nothing true is on the record |
|---|---|---|
| `{first_name}` | contact first name | use "Hi," with no name |
| `{personal_line}` | one true sentence from the record: their role, the practice, or the last thing they did (a post, a talk, a form they filled in) | delete the sentence |
| `{practice_type_line}` | one of the lines below, picked by practice type | delete the sentence |
| `{history_line}` | one of the two variants below | use the cold variant |

### `{history_line}` variants
- **Cold first touch:** `We haven't met. I'm an internist and primary care physician, and I co-founded River Records.`
- **Looked at Stream before** (signed up, booked a demo, or emailed before; the record must show it): `You looked at Stream a while back, so this may not be news. I'm writing myself because the pitch was probably about features, and the reason we built it isn't.`

### `{practice_type_line}` options
- **Primary care / DPC:** `On a full panel, that's the difference between the patients you remember and the rest.`
- **Pediatrics:** `In pediatrics it's the history a family assumes you'll remember from one well visit to the next.`
- **Integrative / complex chronic (adults or children):** `With patients who come to you after a dozen specialists, that picture is most of the work.`
- **SNF / LTC:** `With residents seen by several clinicians across shifts, it's the part that gets lost at handoff.`

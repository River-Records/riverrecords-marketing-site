# Outbound test log

The rules for this log are in `outbound-engine-v1.md`, section 6. It holds **counts only**: no
names, practices, email addresses or quotes from replies. Those stay on the HubSpot card.

Tests run one at a time, in this order: **T1** video vs no video, **T2** the clinician type's own
problem vs the general one, **T3** clinician types compared. Prospects are split A/B before
anyone researches them. A test runs for at least 4 weeks (~100 first emails per version), and
it's read on positive replies first, booked demos second. Never opens.

| Week of | Test | Version | First emails | Replies: positive / not now / no | Unsubscribes | Demos booked | Bounces |
